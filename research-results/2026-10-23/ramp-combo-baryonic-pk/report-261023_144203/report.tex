\begin{abstract}
We report a two-wave computational and literature study of baryonic suppression of the matter power spectrum. Predictions came from a HACC-based hydrodynamics emulator that returns $S(k)=P_{\rm hydro}(k)/P_{\rm grav}(k)$ and the cluster gas fraction $f_{\rm gas}(M_{500c})$ over a seven-parameter design space: five AGN and black-hole parameters plus $\omega_m$ and $\sigma_8$. Nine $z=0$ feedback points were sampled, all at fixed cosmology $\omega_m=0.14$, $\sigma_8=0.8$.

At the centre of the design range, the suppression $1-S$ is $2.2\%$ at $k\simeq1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ and $8.0\%$ at $k\simeq3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. The most suppressed point sampled ($\kappa_w=2$, $e_w=1$, $M_{\rm seed}=0.6$, maximal kinetic feedback) gives $4.4\pm0.3\%$ and $14.8\pm0.9\%$ at these scales. Setting the kinetic-feedback parameters to their minimum removes the suppression almost entirely up to $k=3.7\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. The emulator $k$-grid ends at $\approx12.6\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. For the strong-AGN point, $S(k)$ flattens to $\approx0.789$ at $k\approx11.6$--$12.6\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, so no high-$k$ upturn is resolved. For one extreme point the suppression at $k\simeq3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ falls from $13.0\%$ at $z=0$ to $7.0\%$ at $z=1$.

No sampled point reaches the eROSITA+kSZ preference of $10\pm2\%$ at $k=1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. All are consistent with HSC-XXL ($5\pm4\%$); the significances quoted are contested. Gas fractions at $M_{500c}=1.26\times10^{14}\,h^{-1}M_\odot$ do not separate the fiducial and strong-AGN points, although their $S(k)$ differ.

From the literature read in the session:
\begin{itemize}
\item Reconciling DES Y3 cosmic shear with Planck requires $A_{\rm mod}=0.858\pm0.052$. The corresponding KiDS-1000 value is $A_{\rm mod}\approx0.69$.
\item FLAMINGO finds that baryons are unlikely to drive the $S_8$ tension.
\item A theoretical-error covariance strongly reduces baryon-induced $\Omega_m$ biases in a Euclid-like forecast.
\end{itemize}
No lensing observable was computed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The user asked for four things: a survey of recent work on baryonic suppression of the matter power spectrum; predictions from the available hydrodynamics emulator of the $z=0$ power-spectrum ratio at a few parameter points; an explanation of the AGN-feedback physics behind the suppression; and an assessment of the impact on weak-lensing cosmology. The same request was issued twice. The second issue started a second research wave aimed at the gaps left by the first. These gaps were:
\begin{itemize}
\item the unresolved high-$k$ behaviour of the emulator curves;
\item the maximum suppression reachable within the design range;
\item redshift evolution;
\item the gas-fraction--suppression link;
\item several literature numbers, notably the DES Y3 suppression amplitude and the depth of the FLAMINGO $z=0$ minimum.
\end{itemize}

The scope is a power-spectrum-level study at fixed cosmology. No shear power spectrum $C_\ell$, correlation function $\xi_\pm$, or $S_8$ forecast was computed. The weak-lensing assessment therefore rests on published results.

\section{Methods}
\paragraph{Emulator.} All emulator calls ran locally (dispatch: local execution) through the \texttt{serve-hydro.sh} server. Its tools were \texttt{list\_observables}, \texttt{describe\_parameters}, \texttt{predict\_observable}, \texttt{plot\_prediction} and \texttt{plot\_observable\_comparison}.

The server exposes 14 observables. Two were used:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Pk-ratio}: $S(k)=P_{\rm hydro}(k)/P_{\rm grav}(k)$ on 799 $k$-bins in $h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, trained at $z=0,0.1,0.5,1.0,2.0$.
\item \texttt{fGas}: $M_{\rm gas}/M_{500c}$ within $R_{500c}$ in three bins, $M_{500c}=5.01\times10^{13}$, $7.94\times10^{13}$ and $1.26\times10^{14}\,h^{-1}M_\odot$.
\end{itemize}
The reference spectrum is the emulator's gravity-only $P_{\rm grav}$. Whether it is equivalent to the dark-matter-only spectra used in the literature was not checked.

The design ranges reported by \texttt{describe\_parameters} are listed below. The design is built from 110 simulations in $400\,h^{-1}$Mpc boxes.
\begin{itemize}
\item $\kappa_w\in[2,4]$: AGN wind coupling.
\item $e_w\in[0.2,1.0]$: AGN energy efficiency or wind energy fraction.
\item $M_{\rm seed}\in[0.6,2.0]$: black-hole seed mass, in units of $10^6M_\odot$.
\item $v_{\rm kin}\in[0.1,1.2]$: kinetic feedback velocity, in units of $10^4$ km/s.
\item $\epsilon_{\rm kin}\in[0.02,1.2]$: kinetic efficiency, in units of 10.
\item $\omega_m=\Omega_mh^2\in[0.12,0.155]$.
\item $\sigma_8\in[0.7,0.9]$.
\end{itemize}

The ``fiducial'' point is the centre of the parameter ranges, not a calibrated model. We define the suppression as $1-S(k)$. The quoted uncertainties are the emulator's $1\sigma$ output.

\section{Results}
\subsection{Literature: AGN feedback physics of the suppression}
The mechanism below is assembled from full texts that were read. The critic marked the overall synthesis as contested, so it should be read as the assistant's summary of those texts.
\begin{itemize}
\item Energy injected by AGN redistributes gas relative to the dark matter. This suppresses the non-linear matter power by tens of percent relative to dark-matter-only runs (arXiv:2512.02954).
\item Below about 10 Mpc, AGN push baryons outward. On smaller scales, star formation concentrates baryons in halo centres (arXiv:2309.02920).
\end{itemize}

In FLAMINGO (arXiv:2410.17109; quoted and cited), the following hold:
\begin{itemize}
\item The response is stronger for lower group and cluster gas fractions, for collimated jets instead of thermal AGN feedback, and for lower stellar mass at fixed gas fraction.
\item The response is converged only for volumes above $200^3$ Mpc$^3$.
\item The fiducial minimum of $P/P_{\rm DMO}$ moves from $k=6$ at $z=2$ to $k=10\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $z=0$.
\item The departure from unity begins at $k\sim1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $z=2$ and at $k\sim0.1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $z=0$.
\item Across thermal-AGN variants from $f_{\rm gas}-10\sigma$ to $+4\sigma$, the $z=0$ minimum stays at $k=8$--$10\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. The $f_{\rm gas}$ parameter mainly changes the normalisation, and the response saturates below $f_{\rm gas}-8\sigma$, which is an emulator extrapolation. The jet model gives a stronger response at negative $f_{\rm gas}$ with a similar minimum position.
\item The cosmology dependence is about $1\%$ at $k<10\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\item The FLAMINGO emulator reaches all literature models except Illustris and cosmo-OWLS-AGN-$\Delta T$8.7. Its $f_{\rm gas}-8\sigma$ thermal and jet runs resemble cosmo-OWLS $\Delta T_{\rm AGN}=8.3$ and $8.5$.
\end{itemize}
The paper gives no number for the depth of the $z=0$ minimum; it appears only in its figures. That depth therefore stayed open.

The first-wave survey summary described a 10--30\% minimum at $k\approx5$--$20\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ followed by an upturn. The assistant itself flagged this description as general knowledge not sourced from the papers (arXiv:1104.1174, arXiv:1906.00968).

\paragraph{Observational benchmarks.} Siegel et al. (arXiv:2512.02954) analyse haloes with $13<\log_{10}M_{500}<14$ at $0<z<1$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Joint eROSITA X-ray gas fractions and SDSS/DESI+ACT kSZ profiles give a suppression of $10\pm2\%$ at $k=1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\item HSC-XXL gas fractions give $5\pm4\%$.
\item Only the strong-feedback runs BAHAMAS 8.0 and FLAMINGO $f_{\rm gas}-8\sigma$ agree with the eROSITA+kSZ value.
\item The simulations differ from each other by more than 10\% at $k=1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\end{itemize}

Grandis et al. (arXiv:2309.02920) use clusters above $\sim3\times10^{13}M_\odot$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Their fiducial model gives a suppression of $0.042^{+0.012}_{-0.014}$ at $k=1$ and $0.184^{+0.026}_{-0.031}$ at $k=3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\item Their alternative model gives $0.049$ and $0.179$.
\item At 97.5\% credibility the impact is below 1\% for $k<0.37\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\end{itemize}

These values carry different verification status in the two waves. In the first wave they were stored with verbatim quotes and supported. In the second wave the critic contested their restatements, because the downloaded full texts had not been re-read to confirm them.

\subsection{Emulator: high-$k$ behaviour and redshift evolution}
For the strong-AGN point, $S$ is $0.851$ at $k=5.25$, $0.8205$ at $7.01$, $0.7946$ (std $0.0098$) at $10.01$ and $0.7893$ at $12.55\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, the last tabulated bin. The curve flattens to a plateau of $\approx0.789$--$0.790$ for $k\approx11.6$--$12.6$ and is still decreasing by less than $0.001$ per $0.1\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at the grid edge. The minimum thus sits at or just beyond $k_{\rm max}\approx12.6\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, with a depth of about $21\%$. No upturn lies inside the grid.

For the maxsupp point, $S(k)$ rises from $z=0$ to $z=1$ at every $k$ read:
\begin{itemize}
\item $k=1.013$: from $0.9638\pm0.0020$ to $0.9781\pm0.0018$, so the suppression falls from $3.6\%$ to $2.2\%$.
\item $k=3.007$: from $0.8698\pm0.0065$ to $0.9299\pm0.0055$, so from $13.0\%$ to $7.0\%$.
\item $k=3.70$: from $0.8400\pm0.0076$ to $0.9214\pm0.0064$, so from $16.0\%$ to $7.9\%$.
\end{itemize}
The response therefore grows as $z$ falls, which agrees qualitatively with FLAMINGO. The minimum lies beyond the range read at both redshifts, so its shift could not be measured. The fiducial $z=1$ file was written but not read.

\subsection{Weak-lensing impact (literature)}
\paragraph{KiDS-1000.} Amon \& Efstathiou (arXiv:2206.11794) find that KiDS-1000 shear ($S_8=0.759^{+0.024}_{-0.021}$) is consistent with Planck $\Lambda$CDM if the non-linear power is suppressed through $P_m=P_L+A_{\rm mod}(P_{\rm NL}-P_L)$ with $A_{\rm mod}\approx0.69$. The suppression must extend to $k\sim0.2\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, outside the BAHAMAS range. A later correction on the board notes that this is a sufficient route, not shown to be the only one.

\paragraph{DES Y3.} Preston, Amon \& Efstathiou (arXiv:2305.09827, Eq.~3) give $A_{\rm mod}=0.858\pm0.052$ for DES Y3, with no scale cuts and a Planck prior on $S_8$ and $\Omega_m$. This is weaker than the KiDS value but ``still more extreme than predictions from recent numerical simulations''. Without $A_{\rm mod}$, DES Y3 $S_8$ lies $0.9\sigma$ (variant 3) to $1.8\sigma$ (variant 4) below Planck $S_8=0.828\pm0.016$. The authors note that the suppression need not be baryonic.

\paragraph{FLAMINGO.} FLAMINGO (arXiv:2309.07959) finds it ``unlikely that baryon physics is driving this modification''. Grandis et al. likewise question whether feedback drives the shear--CMB discrepancy.

\paragraph{Mitigation by gas-calibrated priors.} In an LSST-Y1-like mock, Siegel et al. find that ignoring baryons biases $S_8$ significantly; the bias is shown only graphically. An uninformed baryonification model removes the bias but roughly doubles the $S_8$ uncertainty. A kSZ+eROSITA-calibrated prior recovers about half the lost precision.

\paragraph{Mitigation by theoretical-error covariance.} Maraio, Hall \& Taylor (arXiv:2410.12500) replace scale cuts with a theoretical-error covariance on $P(k)$. Their Table~3 uses an idealised Euclid-like survey with $\ell_{\rm max}=5000$; the critic contested this extraction. The values are:
\begin{itemize}
\item Illustris as truth, one-parameter HMCode model, no theory error: $100\,\Delta\Omega_m=-1.317$ ($-5.69\sigma$).
\item The same case with the theory error added: $0.128$ ($0.249\sigma$).
\item BAHAMAS as truth with the one-parameter model: $0.057$ ($0.258\sigma$).
\item EAGLE with the six-parameter model, theory error included: $0.573$ ($1.459\sigma$).
\end{itemize}
No numerical $S_8$ shift was extracted from this paper.

\paragraph{Emulator versus required suppression.} At its sampled points the HACC emulator gives sub-percent suppression at $k\le0.5\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, with $S=0.9967$ at $k=0.212$ for maxsupp\_lowM. The assistant concluded that this is far below the $A_{\rm mod}$-type suppression that must reach $k\sim0.2\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$. This comparison is approximate and contested, and no $A_{\rm mod}$-equivalent $S(k)$ was computed.

\section{Discussion}
\paragraph{What the emulator says about the physics.} Within the HACC design, the suppression is controlled mainly by the kinetic AGN channel ($v_{\rm kin}$, $\epsilon_{\rm kin}$). It is modulated by the wind coupling $\kappa_w$, where lower values give more suppression, and by the seed mass. The dependence on seed mass is not monotonic in an obvious way: $M_{\rm seed}=2$ deepens the suppression relative to the fiducial in the one-at-a-time test, yet $M_{\rm seed}=0.6$ gives the deepest corner. This qualitative picture, feedback-driven suppression growing toward low redshift, matches the FLAMINGO description.

\paragraph{Tension with gas-fraction trends.} The fiducial and strong points have the same group-scale $f_{\rm gas}$ but different $S(k)$. This sits uneasily with the literature view that suppression tracks halo gas fractions. The test used only two points, a single radius ($R_{500c}$) and mass bins below $1.3\times10^{14}\,h^{-1}M_\odot$, and the parameter changes were confounded. It is therefore not evidence against the relation.

\paragraph{Amplitude comparison.} The emulator's sampled amplitudes are compatible with HSC-XXL and, at $k=1$, with Grandis et al. They fall short of the eROSITA+kSZ preference and of the strong suppression invoked to reconcile shear with Planck. A three-to-nine-point corner search cannot establish the emulator's true maximum, as the critic stressed.

\paragraph{Limitations and failures.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Fixed cosmology.} Cosmology was never varied.
\item \textbf{Reference spectrum.} The equivalence of $P_{\rm grav}$ with $P_{\rm DMO}$ was not checked.
\item \textbf{$k$-range.} The grid ends at $\approx12.6\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$, so the minimum and the high-$k$ upturn are not resolved for any point. For most points the curves were only read to $k\approx3.7$ or $7.8$.
\item \textbf{Tool failures.} \texttt{load\_table\_data} failed on all CSVs. \texttt{read\_text\_file} truncated them. Figure extraction from arXiv:2410.17109 failed, so the FLAMINGO minimum depth stayed open. A comparison plot was overwritten.
\item \textbf{Unread files.} Several written outputs were never read: fiducial at $z=1$, and maxsupp at $k=0.2$--$0.3$.
\item \textbf{Unverified quotes.} The second-wave full texts of arXiv:2512.02954, arXiv:2309.02920 and arXiv:2410.17109 were downloaded but not read for verbatim confirmation.
\item \textbf{Survey preamble.} The automated survey text at the start of each wave stated, incorrectly, that no emulator tool was available. Emulator calls were in fact run throughout. Its unsourced shape description should not be read as a finding.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusions}
The session established the following HACC emulator results, all at $\omega_m=0.14$ and $\sigma_8=0.8$:
\begin{itemize}
\item The design-centre point gives $2.2\%$ suppression at $k=1$ and $8.0\%$ at $k=3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $z=0$.
\item The strongest sampled point gives $4.4\pm0.3\%$ and $14.8\pm0.9\%$.
\item Kinetic AGN feedback is responsible for nearly all of the suppression up to $k=3.7\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$.
\item The strong-AGN curve flattens at $S\approx0.789$ near the grid edge ($k\approx12.6\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$) without a resolved upturn.
\item Suppression roughly halves between $z=0$ and $z=1$ at $k\simeq3\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ for the extreme point.
\end{itemize}

From the literature, we sourced:
\begin{itemize}
\item the DES Y3 requirement $A_{\rm mod}=0.858\pm0.052$, against KiDS $A_{\rm mod}\approx0.69$;
\item the FLAMINGO minimum location of $k=8$--$10\,h\,{\rm Mpc}^{-1}$ at $z=0$;
\item the FLAMINGO conclusion that baryons are unlikely to drive the $S_8$ tension;
\item a Euclid-like $\Omega_m$ bias of $-5.7\sigma$ that is reduced to $0.25\sigma$ by a theoretical-error covariance (contested extraction).
\end{itemize}

The board did not rank its remaining open items. In the order they were raised, they are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Verify verbatim the $10\pm2\%$, $5\pm4\%$, $0.042$ and $0.184$ values from the downloaded full texts, and quote the depth of the FLAMINGO $z=0$ minimum and its $S(k=1)$.
\item Make the weak-lensing impact quantitative: obtain a numerical $S_8$ bias with its survey configuration, and compare the HACC $S(k)$ at $k=0.2$--$0.5$ with the $A_{\rm mod}$ requirement.
\item Complete the three-point $f_{\rm gas}$--suppression test including the weak-AGN point.
\item Read the fiducial $z=1$ prediction.
\item Establish whether low $M_{\rm seed}$ combined with $\kappa_w=2$ and maximal kinetic feedback is the true design-range maximum.
\end{enumerate}
