\begin{abstract}
A multi-wave survey of 2024--2026 arXiv work on keV sterile-neutrino dark matter was combined with direct calculations from a sterile-neutrino BBN/X-ray bridge server. BBN observables $Y_{p}$, D/H and $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ were computed over $m_{s}=1$~eV to 100~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-15}$ to $10^{-1}$ for Dodelson--Widrow (DW) and Shi--Fuller (SF) mechanisms, together with radiative decay predictions $E_{\gamma}=m_{s}/2$, $\Gamma$ and lifetime. The server baselines $Y_{p}=0.2449\pm0.004$, D/H$=2.547\pm0.025\times10^{-5}$, $N_{\rm eff,CMB}=2.99\pm0.17$ give a BBN 95\% limit $\Delta N_{\rm eff}<0.548$ ($\Delta\chi^{2}>3.84$). BBN alone allows all keV DW points at X-ray relevant mixings, while built-in NuSTAR M31 and XMM blank-sky limits exclude DW $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ at $m_{s}=6$--20~keV. Non-minimal production with large lepton asymmetry $L\gtrsim0.5$--1 at $T>20$~MeV or inflaton-decay branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$ is required to reach the dark-matter abundance at $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-12}$--$10^{-14}$ where X-ray limits weaken. Structure-formation bounds $m_{\rm th}>6.5$~keV (satellites) and $>6.1$~keV (lensing) translate via $m_{s}=4.43$~keV$(m_{\rm th}/{\rm keV})^{4/3}$ to $m_{s}>53.7$~keV and $>49$~keV for DW, but are colder for resonant and reheating production. The DW normalization in the server is a factor $\sim12$ below the published Abazajian 2017 fit, shifting the $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ contour. No 2024--2026 eROSITA or INTEGRAL/SPI primary line-search paper was retrieved; several X-ray and BBN robustness checks remained incomplete due to tool-budget exhaustion and DNS failures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The session addressed a single overarching question posed repeatedly with evolving emphasis: survey recent arXiv work from 2024 to 2026 on sterile-neutrino dark matter and combine BBN constraints computed with the sterile-nu tools with X-ray decay-line constraints to map viable $(m_{s},\sin^{2}2\theta)$ regions per production mechanism, including structure-formation bounds.

The initial request required using the sterile-neutrino server to compute $Y_{p}$, D/H and $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ together with relic-density constraints across the $(m_{s},\sin^{2}2\theta)$ plane, quantifying X-ray line energies and fluxes and comparing them with 2024--2026 limits from NuSTAR, INTEGRAL/SPI, Chandra, eROSITA and XRISM, surveying production-mechanism literature on Shi--Fuller resonant production with lepton asymmetry, reheating production and scalar decays, and synthesizing a per-mechanism viability table with provenance and robustness checks. Subsequent continuations narrowed the focus to the two weakest claims identified by the research board: the quantitative X-ray limit table at $m_{s}=7.1,10,20,40,50$~keV and the production-mechanism/structure-formation mapping, including the factor $\sim10$--$17$ DW relic-density normalization dispute and the Viel et al. $m_{\rm th}\to m_{s}$ translation, and to BBN robustness under different $Y_{p}$/D/H baselines and thermalization fractions. Later waves explicitly requested deepening the BBN $L$-dependence and the X-ray exclusion of DW-motivated dark matter versus alternative production and field-of-view systematics, and verification of 2024--2026 $m_{\rm th}$ bounds. The scope remained keV sterile neutrinos as warm/cold dark matter, with eV-scale cross-checks for the BEST short-baseline anomaly, and explicitly excluded MeV+ heavy neutral leptons where decay injection is not modeled.

\section{Methods}
All quantitative BBN and X-ray numbers were produced with the Sterile Neutrino $\to$ BBN/X-ray Bridge Server via \texttt{serve-sbbn.sh} on local dispatch, complemented by \texttt{hep-research-aid} and \texttt{local} web and arXiv search tools. The server provides two skills, of which \texttt{sterile-nu-bbn-constraints} was loaded. BBN abundances are from a fit to PRyMordial calibrated to $<0.3\%$, with the only BBN input being $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$. The fit is valid for $N_{\rm eff}$ in $[2.044,6.044]$, $\omega_{b}h^{2}$ in $[0.019,0.0257]$ and $\tau_{n}$ in $[866.4,890.4]$~s, and for $m_{s}\lesssim100$~keV where the sterile is relativistic at BBN. For $m_{s}\gtrsim1$~MeV the server flags \texttt{bbn\_treatment\_valid=false} and decay-product injection is not modeled. Exclusion is $\Delta\chi^{2}>3.84$ versus the Standard Model at 95\% for one degree of freedom. Observational baselines adopted by the server are $Y_{p}=0.2449\pm0.004$ from Aver, Olive and Skillman JCAP 07 (2021) 029, D/H$=2.547\pm0.025\times10^{-5}$ from Cooke, Pettini and Steidel ApJ 855 (2018) 102, $N_{\rm eff,CMB}=2.99\pm0.17$ from Planck 2018 TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing+BAO, and CMB-S4 forecast $3.044\pm0.03$, with Standard Model $N_{\rm eff}=3.044$, $Y_{p}=0.24682$, D/H$=2.4433\times10^{-5}$ and $\chi^{2}=2.77$ at the null. Mechanisms are DW non-resonant and SF resonant, where SF $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ is a heuristic $0.1\times$ DW and lepton asymmetry $L$ is capped at $0.1$ with no $\xi_{e}$ modeling. X-ray limits in the server are ten tabulated NuSTAR/XMM limits across five targets (Perseus, M31, Milky Way center, Coma, stacked galaxies) including NuSTAR M31 Roach et al. 2020 and XMM blank-sky Dessert et al. 2020, evaluated for whole-halo $f_{\rm DM}=1$ with no field-of-view cut. Radiative decay follows $\Gamma=1.38\times10^{-32}\,{\rm s^{-1}}\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-10})(m_{s}/{\rm keV})^{5}$, equivalently $\Gamma=1.38\times10^{-29}\,{\rm s^{-1}}\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-7})(m_{s}/{\rm keV})^{5}$, with $E_{\gamma}=m_{s}/2$.

Computations executed included \texttt{predict\_sterile\_nu} at single points, \texttt{predict\_sterile\_nu\_grid} over masses $[1,3,7.1,10,20,50,100]$~keV and mixings $10^{-15}$ to $10^{-6}$ and over $1$~eV to 100~keV $\times10^{-13}$ to $0.1$, \texttt{predict\_sterile\_nu\_xray\_line} for $E_{\gamma}$, $\Gamma$, lifetime and fluxes, \texttt{scan\_sterile\_nu\_parameters} for 400 to 10000 point scans, \texttt{check\_sterile\_nu\_sbl\_anomaly} for the BEST point, and \texttt{plot\_sterile\_nu\_bbn\_impact} and \texttt{plot\_sterile\_nu\_exclusion} for BBN versus $N_{\rm eff}$ and exclusion-plane figures. Literature searches used \texttt{search\_arxiv\_abstracts}, \texttt{search\_arxiv}, \texttt{get\_arxiv\_metadata}, \texttt{download\_arxiv\_full\_text}, \texttt{read\_arxiv\_chunk}, \texttt{web\_search}, \texttt{get\_paper\_figures} and \texttt{cite}. The supplement holds the full tool-call log and code.

\section{Results}
\subsection{BBN and relic-density allowed region}
The server null ($\sin^{2}2\theta\to0$) predicts $Y_{p}=0.24682$, D/H$\times10^{5}=2.4433$, $N_{\rm eff}=3.0440$, $\chi^{2}=2.77$, $\Delta\chi^{2}=0$, which is $+0.48\sigma$ high in $Y_{p}$ and $-4.15\sigma$ low in D/H versus the server baselines, but allowed at 95\% ($\Delta\chi^{2}<3.84$). Versus the brief baselines $Y_{p}=0.245\pm0.003$, D/H$=2.53\pm0.03\times10^{-5}$ the offsets are $+0.61\sigma$ and $-2.89\sigma$. The offset between brief and server baselines is $0.0001$ in $Y_{p}$ ($0.025\sigma$) and $0.047\times10^{-5}$ in D/H ($1.88\sigma$ on the server error). Linearized slopes about the Standard Model are $dY_{p}/dN_{\rm eff}=0.01351$ and $d{\rm D/H}_{5}/dN_{\rm eff}=0.3308$, where ${\rm D/H}_{5}={\rm D/H}\times10^{5}$. The BBN 95\% limit from $Y_{p}$/D/H alone is $\Delta N_{\rm eff}<0.548$ ($\Delta\chi^{2}$ versus Standard Model $>3.84$), quoted also as $<0.55$ and $<0.61$ depending on baseline and whether $\Delta\chi^{2}$ is versus Standard Model or best fit. A fully thermalized sterile with $N_{\rm eff}=4.044$ ($\Delta N_{\rm eff}=1$) predicts $Y_{p}=0.25956$ and D/H$=2.7769\times10^{-5}$, $3.7\sigma$ above $Y_{p}$, with $\Delta\chi^{2}=23.1$ excluded. Planck $N_{\rm eff}=2.99\pm0.17$ gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}<0.33$ at $1\sigma$ and $<0.61$ at 95\% (approximately $2\sigma$), comparable to BBN, while CMB-S4 $\sigma=0.03$ will give $\Delta N_{\rm eff}<0.06$.

\subsection{Production mechanisms and structure formation}
Four arXiv identifiers were verified as real papers: arXiv:2507.18752 (Vogel, Garcia Escudero, Froustey, Abazajian 2025, Shi--Fuller revival), arXiv:2502.17374 (Gorbunov, Kalashnikov, Krugan 2025, flavor-dependent asymmetry), arXiv:2601.03346 (Cline and Xu 2026, reheating/inflaton-decay with branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$) and arXiv:2609.31340 (Gorbunov and Kalashnikov 2026, saturation at high flavor asymmetry). A contested finding from the full text of arXiv:2507.18752 states $L\gtrsim0.5$ at high temperature opens viable space at $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$, compatible with NuSTAR $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-12}$ at $6$--40~keV and INTEGRAL/SPI $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim3\times10^{-14}$ for $m_{s}\gtrsim40$~keV (lifetimes $\lesssim10^{20}$~s excluded), with structure bounds $m_{\rm th}>8.4$~keV (galaxy counts, 95\% CL), $>8.5$~keV (counts+strong lensing) and Lyman-alpha preference $m_{\rm th}=4.1^{+33.3}_{-1.5}$~keV. The server cannot test this claim: its SF mode caps $L$ at $0.1$, its SF $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ is heuristic $0.1\times$ DW, and asymmetry is not fed into the BBN network. At $10,20,30$~keV with $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-14}$ it gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}\sim10^{-8}$ and $\Delta\chi^{2}=0$ allowed, with $Y_{p}=0.24682$, D/H$=2.4433\times10^{-5}$ at $20$~keV, while SF $\Omega_{s}h^{2}$ values are $0.122,0.490,1.10$ for $L=10^{-3}$ and $1.22,4.90,11.0$ for $L=0.1$, all flagged overclosing, demonstrating the heuristic does not capture resonant enhancement.

DW versus SF BBN contrast is stark: same $(m_{s},\sin^{2}2\theta)$ points BBN-excluded under DW become allowed under SF heuristic. For $m_{s}=7100$~eV, $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-7}$, DW gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.6185$, $\Delta\chi^{2}=5.89$ excluded versus SF $0.06185$, $\Delta\chi^{2}=-0.66$ not excluded; for $100$~eV, $10^{-5}$, DW $1.0$ excluded versus $0.1$ not excluded. In a 24-point eV--keV grid $14/24$ DW points are BBN-excluded ($\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ up to $1.0$, $\Delta\chi^{2}=23.1$) versus $0/24$ SF points at $L=0.01$ and $0.1$. At $20$~keV/$10^{-14}$ DW $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=1.42\times10^{-7}$, $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=3.04\times10^{-5}$ allowed versus SF $1.42\times10^{-8}$, $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=4.90$ overcloses by $40\times$; at $50$~keV/$10^{-14}$ DW $1.58\times10^{-4}$ versus SF $30.6$ overcloses by $255\times$. At $10,20,30$~keV/$10^{-14}$ SF gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=8.1\times10^{-9},1.42\times10^{-8},1.96\times10^{-8}$ allowed but $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=1.22,4.90,11.0$ overclosing, while DW gives $8.1\times10^{-8}$, $8.7\times10^{-6}$ allowed. The server SF $L$ cap $0.1$ therefore cannot test the $L>0.5$ viable region where recent BBN analyses allow $L>1$ at $T>20$~MeV and washout before BBN reopens parameter space.

For DW at small mixing $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-12}$ to $10^{-14}$, $\Omega_{s}h^{2}\ll0.12$ (e.g. $3.5\times10^{-4}$ at $6$~keV/$10^{-12}$ down to $10^{-4}$ at $40$~keV/$10^{-12}$, $3.5\times10^{-6}$ at $6$~keV/$10^{-14}$), so DW cannot make dark matter at X-ray allowed mixing; SF resonant with $L\sim0.1$--1 or inflaton-decay with branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$ is required. Web snippets confirm inflaton-decay produces cold sterile dark matter during reheating at vanishing mixing, probing $T_{\rm RH}$, and that NuSTAR Galactic bulge $190$~ks at $\sim10^{\circ}$ off-plane provides complementary limits to M31 $1.2$~Ms.

Structure-formation bounds from 2024--2026 snippets give thermal-relic $m_{\rm th}>6.5$~keV (Milky Way satellites DES+PS1, Nadler et al. 2024), $>6.1$~keV (strong lensing, 2024) and Lyman-alpha $m_{\rm th}\sim5$--$5.7$~keV. \subsection{Normalization and robustness}
The server DW relic density scales as $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.1\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-9})(m_{s}/3~{\rm keV})^{1.8}$, verified by $3$~keV/$10^{-9}\to0.100$, $10$~keV/$10^{-9}\to0.873$ (ratio $8.73=(10/3)^{1.8}$), but normalization is $3\times$ higher than the brief $0.1\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/3\times10^{-9})(m_{s}/3~{\rm keV})^{1.8}$. The published Abazajian 2017 review arXiv:1705.01837 Eq.~37 fit $m_{s}\sim3.40$~keV$(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-8})^{-0.615}(\Omega_{\rm DM}/0.26)^{0.5}\times0.527\,{\rm erfc}[-1.15\,(T_{\rm QCD}/170~{\rm MeV})^{2.15}]$ inverts to $\sin^{2}2\theta\sim1.23\times10^{-8}$ ($3$~keV), $3.0\times10^{-9}$ ($7.1$~keV), $1.73\times10^{-9}$ ($10$~keV), $5.6\times10^{-10}$ ($20$~keV), $1.26\times10^{-10}$ ($50$~keV) for $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$, while the server gives $1.2\times10^{-9},2.55\times10^{-10},1.37\times10^{-10},3.9\times10^{-11},7.6\times10^{-12}$, a factor $10\times$ ($3$~keV) to $17\times$ ($50$~keV) lower mixing. \section{Discussion}
The combined BBN and X-ray analysis establishes a clear hierarchy: BBN alone is not binding for keV DW dark matter at X-ray relevant mixings, with $\Delta N_{\rm eff}\ll0.548$ and $|\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}|<0.15$ along the $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ contour, while overclosure and X-ray non-detections are binding. The DW $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ line lies at $\sin^{2}2\theta\sim10^{-10}$--$10^{-11}$ (server) or $\sim10^{-9}$--$10^{-10}$ (published), both above NuSTAR $\lesssim10^{-12}$ at $6$--40~keV and SPI $\lesssim3\times10^{-14}$ above $40$~keV, and above XRISM $\Gamma\sim10^{-27}$~s$^{-1}$ ($\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim4\times10^{-11}$--$4\times10^{-10}$ at $7.1$~keV depending on conversion). Structure formation then requires $m_{s}>28$--$76$~keV for DW, where X-ray limits are strongest, leaving no surviving DW all-dark-matter interval. This agrees with the literature consensus that minimal DW is essentially excluded and that non-standard production is required.

Tensions and limitations are dominated by incomplete external validation. Several findings remain contested because verbatim quotes or figure readings were not obtained: the $L\gtrsim0.5$ opening of $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV, $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$ (arXiv:2507.18752), the XRISM $3\sigma$ $\Gamma\sim10^{-27}$~s$^{-1}$ limit (arXiv:2510.24560), and the NuSTAR $6$--40~keV $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-12}$ and SPI $\lesssim3\times10^{-14}$ ceilings. The server SF heuristic ($0.1\times$ DW, $L\le0.1$, no $\xi_{e}$) is unphysical at $L=0.1$ (overclosing by $40$--$255\times$ at $10^{-14}$) and cannot test the $L>0.5$--1 regime where recent BBN analyses allow $L>1$ at $T>20$~MeV to wash out before BBN. The DW normalization offset factor $\sim12$ means server-based DW statements are not conservative; using the published normalization, DW at $7.1$~keV is excluded by XRISM alone.

Dead ends observed include: searches for 2024--2026 eROSITA and INTEGRAL/SPI line limits returned only older or unrelated papers (1505.07829, 1702.08430, muon capture, 2008 heating); targeted INTEGRAL/SPI searches at $m_{s}\ge40$~keV returned no primary 2024+ source, leaving the SPI entry reliant on the arXiv:2507.18752 quote; web searches for XRISM and NuSTAR suffered DNS resolution failures for \texttt{wt.wikipedia.org.cels.anl.gov}; tool budget exhaustion prevented \texttt{read\_arxiv\_chunk}, \texttt{cite}, \texttt{get\_paper\_figures} and additional \texttt{predict\_sterile\_nu\_xray\_line} calls, so no verbatim Abazajian Eq.~37 or Viel coefficient quotes, no numerically read NuSTAR Fig.~3 observed versus expected limits at $7,10,20,40$~keV, no XRISM Fig.~3, no INTEGRAL/SPI primary (Boyarsky diffuse, Roach SPI) to resolve the $50$~keV lifetime mismatch (tool $\tau=7.84\times10^{26}$~s at $3\times10^{-14}$ versus quoted $10^{20}$~s), no corrected five-row X-ray limit table with provenance per mass, no per-mechanism viability table with Viel translation at $7.1/10/20/50$~keV, no exclusion-plane plot distinguishing tool-internal versus literature limits with $\Omega=0.12$ overlay, and no robustness reruns varying $\omega_{b}h^{2}$ and $\tau_{n}$. The critic verdicts reflected this: initial plans were marked revise, subsequent waves continue with contested findings, and final reports were marked revise until the last wave was marked ok.

\section{Conclusions}
Established with high confidence from server calculations is that BBN allows keV sterile-neutrino dark matter at $\sin^{2}2\theta\le10^{-10}$ ($\Delta N_{\rm eff}=6\times10^{-5}$ to $2.9\times10^{-3}$, $\Delta\chi^{2}<0$), that DW overclosure is $40\times$ to $5000\times$ stronger than BBN at $1$--100~keV, that the radiative decay rate follows $\Gamma=1.38\times10^{-32}$~s$^{-1}(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-10})(m_{s}/{\rm keV})^{5}$ to $1$--$2\%$, and that built-in X-ray limits exclude $7.1$~keV/$7\times10^{-11}$, $10$~keV/$10^{-10}$ and $20$~keV/$10^{-10}$ while $40$--$50$~keV/$10^{-10}$ are not excluded by tabulated limits but overclose. The eV BEST point is BBN-excluded ($\Delta\chi^{2}=23.1$) and overcloses. Viable dark matter at X-ray allowed mixings $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-12}$--$10^{-14}$ requires non-minimal production: SF resonant with $L\gtrsim0.5$--1 at $T>20$~MeV (arXiv:2507.18752, arXiv:2512.00793), flavor-dependent asymmetries (arXiv:2502.17374), reheating/inflaton-decay with branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$ (arXiv:2601.03346, arXiv:2601.02069), nuSMEFT freeze-in (arXiv:2409.08968) or time-dependent mass (arXiv:2502.13123), which are colder and relax structure-formation bounds.

What remained open is the quantitative external X-ray limit table with primary figure numbers and verbatim quotes, the factor $\sim12$ DW normalization provenance with Abazajian Eq.~37 text, the Viel $m_{s}=4.43$~keV$(m_{\rm th}/{\rm keV})^{4/3}$ coefficient verbatim, and the joint BBN+X-ray exclusion overlay distinguishing BBN versus built-in versus literature limits.
