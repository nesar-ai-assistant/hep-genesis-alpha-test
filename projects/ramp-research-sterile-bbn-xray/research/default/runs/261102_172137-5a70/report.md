# Sterile-neutrino dark matter: BBN + X-ray joint constraints (2024-2026)

## Abstract
A sterile-neutrino BBN/X-ray bridge server was used to map Dodelson-Widrow (DW) production over m_s = 1 eV to 100 keV and sin^2 2theta = 1e-13 to 0.1, combined with analytic X-ray decay-rate scaling and literature 2024-2026 results. BBN alone allows keV DW dark matter at X-ray-accessible mixings (Delta N_eff << 0.55) but excludes eV-scale thermalized steriles. NuSTAR M31 and XMM blank-sky limits exclude DW benchmarks at 7.1 keV, 7e-11, 10 keV, 1e-11 and 20 keV, 1e-12. XRISM Resolve 3.75 Ms stacking of 10 clusters (2.5-15 keV, no 3.5 keV line) tightens decay-rate limits to Gamma ~1e-27 s^-1 at 7.1 keV. The DW Omega_s h^2 = 0.12 line is excluded as all dark matter when combined with X-ray ceilings and Lyman-alpha m_th bounds, leaving viable dark matter only at sin^2 2theta <= 1e-13 to 1e-14 for m_s = 5-20 keV via non-minimal production (Shi-Fuller resonant with large lepton asymmetry, inflaton decay with BR ~1e-4, nuSMEFT freeze-in, time-dependent mass). A factor ~12 normalization offset between server and literature DW relic density remains not independently verified and determines whether XRISM excludes the DW line.

## Background
Recent 2024-2026 arXiv work converges on minimal DW production being essentially excluded for keV sterile-neutrino dark matter by combined X-ray line searches and structure formation, while non-minimal production reopens viable parameter space.

Key literature identified in the survey brief:

- arXiv:2409.08968 (Sep 2024) shows freeze-in via nuSMEFT higher-dimensional operators can produce the correct relic density across broad m_s, sin^2 2theta where DW fails, defining a target for BBN N_eff versus X-ray comparison.
- arXiv:2502.13123 (Feb 2025) proposes matter-sourced time-dependent mass via an ultralight scalar to evade X-ray decay limits while keeping terrestrial short-baseline hints.
- arXiv:2601.02069 (Jan 2026) systematically maps thermal DW plus non-thermal inflaton decay during reheating with BR ~1e-4, showing viable dark matter at vanishing mixing and how future X-ray limits probe T_RH and inflaton mass.
- arXiv:2512.00793 (Dec 2025) revisits Shi-Fuller resonant production given L_alpha >= 1 allowed at T > 20 MeV, delineating parameter space where flavor asymmetries wash out before BBN/CMB.
- arXiv:2510.19342 (Oct 2025) reports a stacked 3.75 Ms XRISM Resolve search for unidentified lines in 10 galaxy clusters 2.5-15 keV with no 3.5 keV line, placing the strongest high-resolution decay-rate limits for m_s = 5-30 keV.
- arXiv:2405.04568 (Mar 2024) analyzes scalar-mediated sterile self-interactions affecting keV production and free-streaming, relevant to BBN Delta N_eff and structure formation.
- arXiv:2405.17861 (May 2024) presents NuSTAR
