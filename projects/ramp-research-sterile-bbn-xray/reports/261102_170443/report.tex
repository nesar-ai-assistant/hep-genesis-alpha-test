\begin{abstract}
A three-wave interactive session surveyed 2024--2026 arXiv work on keV sterile-neutrino dark matter and combined tool-computed big-bang nucleosynthesis observables ($Y_{p}$, D/H, $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$) with published X-ray radiative-decay limits to map viable $(m_{s},\sin^{2}2\theta)$ regions. The sterile-neutrino BBN/X-ray bridge server was exercised with single-point predictions, product grids and $80\times80$ to $100\times100$ scans in $m_{s}=1$~keV--100~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-15}$--$10^{-6}$, plus X-ray line predictions $E_{\gamma}=m_{s}/2$, $\Gamma$ and fluxes for five targets, and literature figures were extracted. BBN alone does not constrain keV dark matter at X-ray-allowed mixings: along the Dodelson-Widrow dark-matter line $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=10^{-4}$--$1.1\times10^{-2}$ and $|\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}|<0.15$, and the overclosure bound is $40$--$5000$ times stronger than BBN. The Dodelson-Widrow mechanism cannot supply all dark matter; it is excluded by X-ray limits and independently by structure formation. Viable regions quoted in recent literature require resonant Shi-Fuller production with lepton asymmetry $L\gtrsim0.5$ at $T>20$~MeV at $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$, flavor-dependent asymmetries, or non-oscillation reheating production with branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$. The server cannot test the large-$L$ claim: its Shi-Fuller mode caps $L$ at $0.1$ and uses a heuristic $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.1\times$ Dodelson-Widrow value. Tool budget exhaustion left the $7.1/10/20/40/50$~keV X-ray limit table, the Dodelson-Widrow normalization settlement and BBN robustness reruns incomplete, so the joint exclusion remains anchored to prior scans and server heuristics with explicit caveats.
\end{abstract}

\section{Methods}
All quantitative BBN and X-ray numbers were produced with the Sterile Neutrino $\to$ BBN/X-ray Bridge Server (serve-sbbn.sh) under local execution. The server maps $(m_{s},\sin^{2}2\theta,{\rm mechanism})$ to $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$, $N_{\rm eff}$, $\Omega_{s}h^{2}$, $Y_{p}$, D/H, Li/H, $\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}$, $\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}$, lifetime $\tau$, free-streaming scale and BBN-exclusion and overclosure flags, and to radiative-decay line energy $E_{\gamma}=m_{s}/2$, rate $\Gamma$ and flux for Perseus, M31, Milky Way center, Coma and stacked galaxies. BBN abundances come from a fit to the PRyMordial network ($<0.3\%$ error) whose only BBN input is $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$. The fit is calibrated for $N_{\rm eff}\in[2.044,6.044]$, $\omega_{b}h^{2}\in[0.019,0.0257]$ and $\tau_{n}\in[866.4,890.4]$~s and is valid for $m_{s}\lesssim100$~keV where the sterile is relativistic at BBN; for $m_{s}\gtrsim1$~MeV decay-product injection is not modeled and results are flagged not as constraints. Exclusion is $\Delta\chi^{2}>3.84$ (95\% for one degree of freedom) versus the Standard Model. Observational baselines hard-coded in the server are $Y_{p}=0.2449\pm0.004$ from Aver, Olive and Skillman JCAP 07 (2021) 029, D/H$=(2.547\pm0.025)\times10^{-5}$ from Cooke, Pettini and Steidel ApJ 855 (2018) 102, $N_{\rm eff,CMB}=2.99\pm0.17$ from Planck 2018 TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing+BAO, Standard Model $N_{\rm eff}=3.044$ and CMB-S4 forecast $3.044\pm0.03$. X-ray limits in the server are ten tabulated NuSTAR and XMM-Newton limits across the five targets, evaluated as whole-halo $f_{\rm DM}=1$ with no field-of-view cut. Mechanisms are Dodelson-Widrow non-resonant and Shi-Fuller resonant, where the Shi-Fuller $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ is a heuristic $0.1\times$ the Dodelson-Widrow value and the relic density is a fitting formula in $L$, capped at $L=0.1$ with no $\xi_{e}$ modeling; non-thermal reheating and inflaton-decay production with branching ratio $\sim10^{-4}$, reheating temperature and inflaton mass are not modeled.

\section{Results}
\subsection{First wave: literature survey and BBN plus relic-density mapping}
Four arXiv identifiers were resolved by metadata lookup to real papers: arXiv:2507.18752 (Vogel, Garcia Escudero, Froustey and Abazajian 2025, Shi-Fuller revival), arXiv:2502.17374 (Gorbunov, Kalashnikov and Krugan 2025, flavor-dependent asymmetry), arXiv:2601.03346 (Cline and Xu 2026, reheating and inflaton-decay production with branching ratio $\lesssim10^{-4}$) and arXiv:2609.31340 (Gorbunov and Kalashnikov 2026, saturation of resonant production at high flavor asymmetry). Only arXiv:2507.18752 was read in full text; the other three were seen as abstracts, so their claims are reported as abstract-level. A contested finding from the full text states that lepton asymmetries $L\gtrsim0.5$ at high temperature open significant viable parameter space at $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$, compatible with X-ray constraints from NuSTAR at $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-12}$ in $6$--$40$~keV and from INTEGRAL/SPI at lifetimes below $\sim10^{20}$~s excluded at 95\% corresponding to $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim3\times10^{-14}$ above $\sim40$~keV, and with structure-formation bounds $m_{\rm th}>8.4$~keV from galaxy counts at 95\% and $m_{\rm th}>8.5$~keV from counts plus strong lensing, while also quoting a Lyman-$\alpha$ preference $m_{\rm th}=4.1^{+33.3}_{-1.5}$~keV and that the original Dodelson-Widrow mechanism is ruled out as producing all dark matter. A second contested finding maps mechanisms from literature quotes: Shi-Fuller with $L\gtrsim0.5$ above $20$~MeV viable at $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$ with the NuSTAR and SPI ceilings above, flavor-dependent asymmetries with inverted ordering opening lower mixings and colder spectra that reopen lighter $m_{s}$, large $L$ saturating resonant production, and reheating production opening new regions at very small mixing.

The server's radiative decay rate was verified against $\Gamma=1.38\times10^{-32}\,{\rm s^{-1}}\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-10})(m_{s}/{\rm keV})^{5}$ to within about $1.5\%$. At $m_{s}=7.1$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-10}$ the tool gives $\Gamma=2.46\times10^{-28}$~s$^{-1}$ versus $2.49\times10^{-28}$~s$^{-1}$ from the formula with $E_{\gamma}=3.55$~keV; at $20$~keV and $10^{-12}$ it gives $4.36\times10^{-28}$~s$^{-1}$ versus $4.42\times10^{-28}$~s$^{-1}$ with $E_{\gamma}=10$~keV. The tool's built-in X-ray limits from NuSTAR M31 (Roach et al. 2020) and XMM-Newton blank sky (Dessert et al. 2020) exclude both points.

A Dodelson-Widrow benchmark grid at $m_{s}=7.1,10,20,50$~keV with $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-10},10^{-12},10^{-14}$ shows no BBN exclusion: $\Delta N_{\rm eff}\le2.9\times10^{-3}$ and $|\Delta\chi^{2}|\le0.04$. The relic density reaches $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ only near $\sin^{2}2\theta\sim10^{-10}$ at $10$--$20$~keV ($0.087$ at $10$~keV, $0.30$ at $20$~keV which overcloses, $1.58$ at $50$~keV and $10^{-10}$); at X-ray-allowed mixings $10^{-12}$--$10^{-14}$ the Dodelson-Widrow density is $\le0.016$, so a non-Dodelson-Widrow mechanism is needed for all dark matter.

A $100\times100$ Dodelson-Widrow scan from $1$--$100$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-15}$--$10^{-6}$ finds BBN at $\Delta\chi^{2}>3.84$ excludes only $\sin^{2}2\theta$ above $3.5\times10^{-7}$ at $1$~keV, $1.2\times10^{-7}$ at $3.7$~keV, $8.1\times10^{-8}$ at $7$~keV, $4.3\times10^{-8}$ at $13.5$~keV, $1.5\times10^{-8}$ at $50$~keV and $10^{-8}$ at $100$~keV. The $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.12$ contour lies at $\sin^{2}2\theta=8.7\times10^{-9}$ at $1$~keV, $2.5\times10^{-9}$ at $2$~keV, $1.2\times10^{-9}$ at $3$~keV, $4.8\times10^{-10}$ at $5$~keV, $2.55\times10^{-10}$ at $7.1$~keV, $1.37\times10^{-10}$ at $10$~keV, $3.9\times10^{-11}$ at $20$~keV, $7.6\times10^{-12}$ at $50$~keV and $2.2\times10^{-12}$ at $100$~keV, where $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=10^{-4}$--$1.1\times10^{-2}$ and $|\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}|<0.15$. The overclosure bound is therefore $40$ times stronger than BBN at $1$~keV and about $5000$ times stronger at $100$~keV, and BBN leaves open all of the parameter space that Dodelson-Widrow dark matter actually occupies.

Tool-computed BBN at representative points, compared with the brief's $Y_{p}=0.245\pm0.003$ and D/H$=(2.53\pm0.03)\times10^{-5}$, gives Standard Model $N_{\rm eff}=3.044$ with $Y_{p}=0.246815$ ($+0.6\sigma$) and D/H$=2.4433\times10^{-5}$ ($-2.9\sigma$). At $7.1$~keV and $3\times10^{-10}$, $Y_{p}=0.24684$, D/H$=2.4440\times10^{-5}$ and $\Delta\chi^{2}=-0.024$; at $1$~keV and $3\times10^{-7}$, $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.387$, $Y_{p}=0.25193$ ($+2.3\sigma$), D/H$=2.5718\times10^{-5}$ ($+1.4\sigma$) and $\Delta\chi^{2}=+0.44$ allowed; at $10$~keV and $10^{-7}$, $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.813$, $Y_{p}=0.25730$ ($+4.1\sigma$), D/H$=2.7145\times10^{-5}$ ($+6.2\sigma$) and $\Delta\chi^{2}=13.4$ excluded. The server's Dodelson-Widrow relic density scales as $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.1\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/10^{-9})(m_{s}/3\,{\rm keV})^{1.8}$, checked at $3$~keV and $10^{-9}$ giving $0.100$ and at $10$~keV and $10^{-9}$ giving $0.873$ for ratio $8.73=(10/3)^{1.8}$, but the normalization is three times higher than the brief's $0.1\,(\sin^{2}2\theta/3\times10^{-9})(m_{s}/3\,{\rm keV})^{1.8}$, so the server's dark-matter line sits at three times smaller mixing.

The server cannot directly test the large-$L$ Shi-Fuller claim. Its Shi-Fuller mode caps lepton asymmetry at $0.1$ while the paper needs $L\gtrsim0.5$, its $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ is a heuristic $0.1\times$ the Dodelson-Widrow value, and the asymmetry is not fed into the BBN network. At $m_{s}=10,20,30$~keV with $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-14}$ it gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}\sim10^{-8}$ and $\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}=0$ allowed, with $Y_{p}=0.24682$ and D/H$=2.4433\times10^{-5}$ at $20$~keV, while $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.122,0.490,1.10$ for $L=10^{-3}$ and $1.22,4.90,11.0$ for $L=0.1$, all flagged as overclosing.

A cross-check at the BEST short-baseline best fit $m_{s}=1.14$~eV and $\sin^{2}2\theta=0.42$ gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=1$ ($N_{\rm eff}=4.044$), $Y_{p}=0.25956$, D/H$=2.777\times10^{-5}$ and $\Delta\chi^{2}_{\rm BBN}=23.1$ excluded, $6.2\sigma$ tension with Planck $N_{\rm eff}=2.99\pm0.17$ and $33\sigma$ at CMB-S4, with $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=29.3$ under Dodelson-Widrow.

Two contested X-ray findings were recorded. XRISM Resolve stacked $3.75$~Ms on ten galaxy clusters (arXiv:2510.24560) and found no unidentified lines over $2.5$--$15$~keV; its $3\sigma$ upper limit on the decay rate for $m_{s}\sim7.1$~keV ($E=3.55$~keV) is $\Gamma\sim1.0\times10^{-27}$~s$^{-1}$, $3$--$4$ times stronger than Hitomi Perseus but still five times above the Bulbul et al. 2014 XMM-Newton detection, so XRISM does not yet rule out the $3.55$~keV line, scaling to $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim4\times10^{-10}$ at $7.1$~keV from the server rate $2.46\times10^{-28}$~s$^{-1}$ at $10^{-10}$. A contested final viability table states Dodelson-Widrow is not viable as all dark matter with the dark-matter line at $\sin^{2}2\theta=2.55\times10^{-10}$ at $7.1$~keV, $1.37\times10^{-10}$ at $10$~keV, $3.9\times10^{-11}$ at $20$~keV and $7.6\times10^{-12}$ at $50$~keV (server normalization) all above XRISM, NuSTAR and SPI limits, binding constraint X-ray while BBN gives $\Delta\chi^{2}<0.15$ along the line; Shi-Fuller with $L\gtrsim0.5$ is quoted viable at $m_{s}\gtrsim10$~keV and $\sin^{2}2\theta\lesssim10^{-14}$; flavor-dependent asymmetries and reheating production open further regions but rest on abstracts only.

The $3.5$~keV benchmark $m_{s}=7.1$~keV, $\sin^{2}2\theta=7\times10^{-11}$, $E_{\gamma}=3.55$~keV is flagged excluded by built-in NuSTAR M31 and XMM-Newton blank-sky limits with whole-halo fluxes $3.28$~ph~s$^{-1}$~cm$^{-2}$ Milky Way center, $4.8\times10^{-4}$ M31, $2.5\times10^{-5}$ Perseus and $2.8\times10^{-5}$ Coma and $\tau=5.82\times10^{27}$~s.

A Dodelson-Widrow grid of seven masses $1,3,7.1,10,20,50,100$~keV times ten mixings $10^{-15}$--$10^{-6}$ yields $12/70$ BBN-excluded and $31/70$ overclosing, with $1$~keV and $10^{-6}$ giving $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=1.0$, $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=13.84$ and $\Delta\chi^{2}=23.1$ excluded while $7.1$~keV and $10^{-10}$ gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=6.2\times10^{-4}$ and $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.047$ allowed. A $6400$-point scan $1$--$100$~keV and $10^{-15}$--$10^{-6}$ yields $862/6400$ BBN-excluded and $2752/6400$ overclosing, with BBN $95\%$ upper limit $\sin^{2}2\theta_{\max}$ falling from $3.5\times10^{-7}$ at $1$~keV to $8.9\times10^{-9}$ at $100$~keV ($1.23\times10^{-7}$ at $3.6$~keV, $7.26\times10^{-8}$ at $6.85$~keV, $4.29\times10^{-8}$ at $13.8$~keV, $2.54\times10^{-8}$ at $26.2$~keV, $1.5\times10^{-8}$ at $49.7$~keV). At $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-10}$ the X-ray line predictions at $10$~keV ($E_{\gamma}=5$~keV, $\Gamma=1.36\times10^{-27}$~s$^{-1}$, $\tau=7.35\times10^{26}$~s, Perseus flux $1.4\times10^{-4}$~ph~s$^{-1}$~cm$^{-2}$) and $20$~keV ($E_{\gamma}=10$~keV, $\Gamma=4.36\times10^{-26}$~s$^{-1}$, $\tau=2.30\times10^{25}$~s, Perseus flux $2.24\times10^{-3}$) are excluded by NuSTAR M31 plus XMM-Newton, while $40$~keV ($E_{\gamma}=20$~keV, $\Gamma=1.39\times10^{-24}$~s$^{-1}$, $\tau=7.18\times10^{23}$~s, Perseus flux $3.58\times10^{-2}$) is not excluded by tabulated limits within $30\%$ in mass. Combined BBN plus X-ray viability at $\sin^{2}2\theta=10^{-10}$ is therefore BBN-allowed with $\Delta\chi^{2}=-0.008$ to $-0.038$ and $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.0006$--$0.0029$ for all five masses but X-ray-excluded at $7.1/10/20$~keV, overclosing at $40/50$~keV; at $10^{-12}$ all masses are BBN-allowed and not overclosing with $\Omega_{s}h^{2}=0.00047$--$0.0158$ and $\tau=4.07\times10^{29}$--$2.35\times10^{25}$~s requiring external $2024$--$2026$ limits to assess exclusion; at $10^{-14}$ all masses are far below dark-matter abundance and BBN limits.
